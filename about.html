About Equip Solutions Group

An independent sales representative for SwapLoader and StarMix, and a parts and equipment sourcing desk for everything those two lines do not cover.

Paul Devine

The person on the other end

Equip Solutions Group is built around a simple arrangement: you get a direct line to someone who knows the equipment, has a stake in the outcome, and will still take your call two years after the invoice cleared.

Representing manufacturers means learning their products in far more detail than a catalog shows — which models are worth the upcharge, where the lead times really sit, and which spec decisions people regret. Working independently means being able to say all of that out loud.

The work splits three ways: hooklift systems, volumetric concrete plants, and a sourcing desk for used equipment and parts. They look unrelated until you notice the common thread — every one of them is about a machine that has to be earning instead of parked.

How I work

Four commitments, and what they cost me if I break them

These are not values on a wall. They are the reasons people call back.

01

Fit before sale

If a smaller unit, a different model or a used machine serves you better, that is the recommendation. A wrong sale costs more in referrals than it makes in commission.

02

Real numbers

Lead times and freight as they actually are, not as they would sound best. When something slips upstream, you hear it from me before you notice it yourself.

Including after the sale, including on a part worth forty dollars, including when the answer is that I cannot help and here is who can.

How it works

What independent means here

  • I represent the manufacturers, I do not build the equipment. Warranty, engineering and production sit with the factory. My job is spec, quote, order and support.
  • I am paid on what I sell. Which is exactly why a wrong-fit sale is bad business — the referral is worth more than the ticket.
  • Not every request fits a line I carry. When that happens I either source it independently or point you to whoever can actually help.
