The case for a hooklift

Buy bodies, not trucks

A second body costs a fraction of a second truck — and it does not need a driver, a plate, insurance or a parking spot. A hooklift turns one chassis into whatever the week demands, and the swap takes minutes from the cab.

  • Hauling and dumping without dedicating a truck to either.
  • Drop and go. Leave the container on site and keep the chassis working.
  • Seasonal swing. Plow and spreader in winter, dump and flatbed the rest of the year.
  • Non-CDL options. The light-duty models open the Class 4–6 market without a CDL driver.

Before you order

Five things that decide the model

Get these right on the phone and the build goes clean. Get them wrong and the hoist shows up correct on paper and useless in the yard.

  • Chassis CA and wheelbase. Determines which hoists physically mount and how the body sits.
  • Body length — existing and future. If you already own containers, the hoist has to match them, not the other way round.
  • Hook height. 36″, 54″ or 62″ changes what bodies you can pick up. Dual-height options exist for a reason.
  • Real loaded weight. Capacity ratings are for the loaded body, not the empty one. Scrap and aggregate get heavy fast.
  • Who is upfitting. Shop scheduling is often the longest item in the lead time. We plan around it up front.
